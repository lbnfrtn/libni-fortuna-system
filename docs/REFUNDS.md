# Refund & Cancellation Terms — ✅ APPROVED

**Status: ✅ Approved by Libni, 2026-09-17.** The client-facing lines below are now live in `config/offers.ts` (`refundNote`) and show at checkout; use the same wording in the GHL/Documents agreements. To change any of them later, edit the offer's `refundNote` and update the matching agreement.

Two versions per offer: a short **client-facing line** (goes on the checkout page + agreement) and a **note** explaining the thinking so you can decide.

General principles I used:
- Deposits and reserved 1:1 time / retreat seats are **non-refundable** — because they cost you the moment they're booked (you turned others away).
- Prefer **rescheduling or transferring** over refunding — kinder, and keeps the relationship.
- **Payment-plan instalments already paid are not refunded**; remaining instalments stop if you both agree to end early.
- Deep transformational work isn't returnable like a product once it's begun — but people's lives genuinely change, so build in a pause.

## Ignite — ₱7,777, 90-min session  ✅
**Client-facing:** *Life happens — you can reschedule once with at least 48 hours' notice. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed, aren't refundable, but let's talk if something serious came up.*
**Note:** Low price, your time is the cost. One free reschedule keeps it warm; no cash refunds keeps it simple.

## Private Studio Sessions — ₱7,999/person  ✅
**Client-facing:** *Because your space and time are reserved just for your group, payment is non-refundable within 7 days of the session. Before that, you can reschedule or transfer your spot to someone else.*
**Note:** In-person, space is booked. Transfer option is the kind way out.

## Workshops & Trainings — per event  ✅
**Client-facing:** *Your spot is transferable to someone else any time. Refunds are available up to 14 days before the event; after that it's non-refundable but still transferable.*
**Note:** Standard event terms. Adjust the 14 days to your booking lead time.

## The Becoming — ₱250,000, 12-week 1:1  ✅
**Client-facing:** *This is a mutual 12-week commitment. There's a 3-day window after signing to change your mind for a full refund. Once we've begun, the fee is non-refundable — but if life makes continuing impossible, we can pause for up to [8 weeks] and resume. On a payment plan, completed payments aren't refunded and the remaining balance is still due unless we agree together to close early.*
**Note:** This protects a big block of your calendar. The 3-day cooling-off is fair and reduces buyer's remorse disputes. The pause is your humane escape valve. **Decide: is the balance always due, or forgiven if you both agree to stop? I drafted "due unless agreed."**

## Liberate — group coaching  ✅
**Client-facing:** *You can request a full refund up to 7 days before the group begins. Once the cohort has started, the fee is non-refundable because your place shaped the group — but you're welcome to carry your spot to a future cohort if you need to step away.*
**Note:** Cohort-based; late drops hurt the group. Transfer-to-next-cohort is generous and keeps them.

## Essence Retreat — 4-day retreat  ✅
**Client-facing:** *A 30% deposit holds your seat and is non-refundable (it's how we reserve the space and prepare for you). The balance is due 30 days before the retreat. Cancel more than 30 days out and your balance is refunded; within 30 days it's non-refundable, but your full payment can transfer to the next retreat once. Waivers and health forms must be completed to attend.*
**Note:** This is the spec default (30% non-refundable deposit, balance 30 days prior). Retreats have real fixed costs. The one-time transfer is the mercy clause.

## Founders Circle — per seat  ✅
**Client-facing:** *Seats are limited, so payment is non-refundable — but fully transferable to a guest if you can't make it.*
**Note:** Small recurring dinner; transfer is the natural answer.

## Private Experiences & Retreats (custom)  ✅
**Client-facing:** *Terms are set in your agreement. A 50% deposit confirms and is non-refundable; the balance follows the schedule in your agreement.*
**Note:** Bespoke — the signed agreement governs.

## Corporate / Speaking / Brands (track D)  ✅
**Client-facing:** *Governed by the signed agreement. A 50% deposit confirms the date and is non-refundable. Cancellation within [30] days of the date forfeits the deposit; the balance is due per the agreement.*
