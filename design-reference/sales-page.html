Why I created Liberate

If you’re reading this, I want you to know I see you. I know that feeling.

For years, I carried so much that wasn’t mine: the weight of expectations, emotional baggage I didn’t even realize I was holding, and patterns that kept me stuck in cycles I desperately wanted to break.

I did all the things. The self-help books. The affirmations. The journaling. And while they helped, I still felt like something was missing. Like there was something deeper blocking me from fully stepping into who I was meant to be.

It wasn’t until I went beyond mindset work and into deep subconscious and energetic healing that everything changed.

I started working with my chakras, rewiring my subconscious mind, releasing stored trauma, and integrating the spiritual side of healing. And for the first time, I felt free.

That’s why I created Liberate. Because I know you’ve done the work, but something still isn’t clicking. And I want to take you beyond surface-level healing into the deep, soul-shifting work that truly sets you free.

If you’re ready for that, welcome home. This is your space.
